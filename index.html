Explore WFP together

This independent educational project introduces the World Food Programme to children aged 8–12. It is based on WFP’s official information, with simplified explanations and fictional quiz situations.

Talk about what you learn

  • How can a school meal help a child, a family and a farmer?
  • Why might one community need food deliveries while another needs cash support?
  • Why should local people help shape the response?

Getting involved

Children are not responsible for solving hunger. Help them learn, ask questions and choose suitable activities. Any donation or fundraising should be guided by a grown-up using verified official channels.
